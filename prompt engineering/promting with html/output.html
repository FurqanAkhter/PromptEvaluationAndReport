# One-Day Carbohydrate Loading Meal Plan for Marathon Race

Here is a one-day meal plan for an athlete aiming to carbohydrate load for a marathon race tomorrow:

* **Calorie Target:** Approximately 3,500 calories
* **Macronutrient Breakdown:** Protein (130g), Fat (90g), Carbs (520g)

**Meal Plan:**

* **Breakfast (7:00 AM):** Pancakes (3 large, 150g) with honey (40g) and banana slices (100g). Scrambled eggs (2 eggs, 100g) and orange juice (240ml).
  * Protein: 20g, Fat: 18g, Carbs: 95g

* **Mid-Morning Snack (10:00 AM):** Bagel (85g) with cream cheese (30g) and jam (30g). Sports drink (500ml, 6% carbs).
  * Protein: 12g, Fat: 12g, Carbs: 95g

* **Lunch (1:00 PM):** Pasta with marinara sauce (250g cooked pasta, 150g sauce) and ground turkey (120g). Side salad with olive oil dressing (100g greens, 15ml oil).
  * Protein: 35g, Fat: 18g, Carbs: 120g

* **Afternoon Snack (4:00 PM):** Rice cakes (4 cakes, 80g) with peanut butter (40g) and honey (30g). Apple (180g).
  * Protein: 15g, Fat: 20g, Carbs: 95g

* **Dinner (7:00 PM):** White rice (200g cooked) with grilled chicken breast (150g) and steamed carrots (150g) with butter (15g). Garlic bread (2 slices, 60g).
  * Protein: 42g, Fat: 15g, Carbs: 95g

* **Evening Snack (9:30 PM):** Muffin (100g) with jam (25g) and a glass of milk (240ml, 2%).
  * Protein: 8g, Fat: 7g, Carbs: 55g
